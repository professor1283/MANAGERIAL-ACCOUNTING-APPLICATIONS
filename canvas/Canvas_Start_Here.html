Northbridge Components MBA Master Budget Simulation

This simulation is used throughout the semester. Your instructor will provide a secure course-specific launch link. Students sign in with the exact name shown on the class roster. On first access, each student creates a five-digit numerical password and uses the same password thereafter.

Recommended: Use the Canvas module item that opens the simulation in a new browser tab. Your drafts and graded attempts are saved in the class semester database, so you may return to the same work later.

If your instructor has provided the portable launcher, download Student Semester Launcher. Open it on your computer, enter the secure simulation address once, and use it to return to the simulation during the semester.

Student support and grading: Each budget section provides a free Detailed Explanation button, an Answer Hint / Assistance button that applies your instructor's configured penalty the first time it is used in that section, and a one-time Check My Work button when enabled by your instructor; its penalty is also instructor-configured. Your current cumulative grade and penalty total are shown in the workspace.

After you submit a graded attempt, open Results and select Download Grade PDF for Canvas. Save that PDF on your computer and upload it to the Canvas assignment when your instructor requires grade documentation.

Remember the five-digit numerical password you create on first access. If you have access problems, contact your instructor.
