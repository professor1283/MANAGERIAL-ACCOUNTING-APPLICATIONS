Northbridge Components MBA Master Budget Simulation

This simulation is used throughout the semester. Your instructor will provide your simulation username/password and a secure course-specific launch link.

Recommended: Use the Canvas module item that opens the simulation in a new browser tab. Your drafts and graded attempts are saved in the class semester database, so you may return to the same work later.

If your instructor has provided the portable launcher, download Student Semester Launcher. Open it on your computer, enter the secure simulation address once, and use it to return to the simulation during the semester.

Do not delete or replace your course login information. If you have access problems, contact your instructor.
