NORTHBRIDGE COMPONENTS MBA BUDGET SIMULATION — STUDENT LAUNCHER

1. Open Student_Semester_Launcher.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
2. If your instructor has not already configured the address, enter the instructor-provided HTTPS simulation address once.
3. Click Open Simulation. Enter your full name exactly as it appears on the class roster. The first time you enter, create a five-digit numerical password. Use the same five digits every time you return.

The launcher stores only the simulation web address. It does not store your password or your accounting work.
Inside the simulation, your entries are protected automatically in the browser and synchronized to the semester database whenever a connection is available.
Use the Recovery Tools inside the simulation if you want to download an additional backup of your current work.


STUDENT SUPPORT AND GRADE PDF
- Detailed Explanation: no grade penalty.
- Answer Hint / Assistance: first use in each section applies the percentage-point penalty configured by your professor.
- Check My Work: when enabled by your professor, may be used once per section and applies the professor-configured percentage-point penalty.
- The Budget Workspace displays the current cumulative grade, raw score, penalty points, and completion count.
- After submitting, open Results and choose Download Grade PDF for Canvas. Save the PDF and upload it to Canvas when requested by your instructor.
